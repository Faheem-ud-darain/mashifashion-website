Size Guide

Find your perfect fit. Our garments are designed with a contemporary, architectural silhouette. Use the charts below to determine the best size for your style.

How to Measure

1. Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal.

2. Waist

Measure around the narrowest part (typically where your body bends side to side).

3. Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips, keeping the tape horizontal.

Still unsure? Contact our tailors for a personalized size recommendation.
